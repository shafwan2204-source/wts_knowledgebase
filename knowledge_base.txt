### Child Half Twin (CHT) Rooming & Pricing Guide
* Core Rule: Child Half Twin (CHT) applies when a child shares a standard twin room with 1 Adult, occupying the second adult bed space instead of using an extra rollaway or sharing existing bedding. Therefore, CHT is always paired with 1 Adult Twin.
* Rooming Scenarios:
  * Scenario A: 1 Adult + 1 Child (1A1C)
    * 1 Room: (1 Adult Twin + 1 Child CHT)
  * Scenario B: 1 Adult + 2 Children (1A2C)
    * 1 Room: (1 Adult Twin + 1 Child CHT) + 1 Child (CWB or CNB)
  * Scenario C: 2 Adults + 1 Child (2A1C)
    * Option 1 (1 Room): (2 Adults Twin + Twin) + 1 Child CWB
    * Option 2 (2 Rooms): Room 1 (1 Adult Twin + 1 Child CHT) + Room 2 (1 Adult Single)
  * Scenario D: 2 Adults + 2 Children (2A2C)
    * Option 1 (1 Room): (2 Adults Twin + Twin) + 1 Child CWB + 1 Child CNB
    * Option 2 (2 Rooms): Room 1 (1 Adult Twin + 1 Child CHT) + Room 2 (1 Adult Twin + 1 Child CHT)
* Key Definitions:
  * CHT: Child Half Twin (occupies 1 standard adult bed slot in a twin room).
  * CWB: Child With Bed (child in extra bed / rollaway).
  * CNB: Child No Bed (child sharing existing bed without extra bed).

### Official Cruise Websites

* Princess Cruises: https://www.princess.com/

### Halal & Muslim-Friendly Food Options on Cruises
* Dream Cruises: Halal dining is available at The Lido (Halal-Certified restaurant).
* Star Voyager: Halal dining is available at The Lido (Halal-Certified restaurant).
* Royal Caribbean: Offers Halal meat options on sailings departing from Singapore and Dubai. *Note to Agent*: Halal meat does NOT mean the entire meal or kitchen preparation is fully Halal-certified.
* Disney Cruise Line: Muslim-friendly, but not fully Halal. Halal meat options are available; passengers can pre-request Halal meat directly via their Disney Cruise App.

### Cruise Casino Operating Rules & Availability
* Operating Hours / Regulations: The shipboard casino only opens while the cruise ship is sailing in international waters. Whenever the ship is berthed or docked at a port/harbour, the casino remains strictly closed.
* Ship Casino Availability by Brand:
  * Casinos Onboard: Dream Cruises, Star Voyager, Star Navigator, and Royal Caribbean all feature onboard casinos.
  * No Casino Onboard: Disney Cruise Line does NOT have a casino.

* Genting Dream / Resorts World Cruises: 50+ years old per cabin[cite: 1, 2].

### Mandatory Online Check-In

* China: Mandarin speaking.

* Korea: Mandarin speaking (unless otherwise stated in itinerary/sales kit).

* Philippines: English speaking guide.
* Thailand: English speaking guide (unless otherwise stated in itinerary/sales kit).
* Vietnam: Mandarin or English speaking guide depending on departure date (strictly refer to sales kit / itinerary, as certain departures are English and others are Chinese).

* Bhutan: English speaking tour guide.
