### Missing Information & Unlisted Inquiries

* Action: Contact the Team Lead for clarification before replying or committing to the customer[cite: 3].

### Agent Verification & Discretion
* Agents are required to critically evaluate all AI-provided responses rather than accepting them as absolute truth[cite: 3].
* If there is any major doubt, inconsistency, or missing operational policy, agents must not guess, extrapolate, or proceed unassisted[cite: 3].
* Action: Immediately escalate and clarify with the Team Lead before giving any final confirmation to the customer[cite: 3].

### TBF Referral & Internal Department Non-Response Escalations
* Tour Booking Form (TBF): If a customer holds a Tour Booking Form (TBF), inquiries, issues, or follow-ups must always be referred back to the Main Escalation Group[cite: 3].
* Internal Department Non-Response: If internal operating departments (such as Coach, MICE, Dream Ride, etc.) do not reply or fail to respond, agents must always escalate directly to the Team Lead[cite: 3].

  * Any amendments, date/time changes, cancellations, or refund requests must always be referred back to the respective purchasing platform first[cite: 3].
  * The call centre cannot directly cancel, modify, or refund bookings made through external partner channels[cite: 3].

### CASE (Consumers Association of Singapore) Enquiries
* Immediately escalate all correspondence involving CASE to management, regardless of whether it was previously escalated[cite: 3].

### Customer Complaints Handling
* Mandatory Information to Collect[cite: 3]:
  * Full Name[cite: 3]

  * Customer unable to check into hotel, cruise, or tour[cite: 3].

## 4. BOOKING POLICIES & FARE RULES

### Child & Infant Age Definitions and Policies
* General Age Categories (Overland & Overseas Packages):
  * Child: 2 years old to 11 years old.
  * Infant: Below 2 years old.
* Overland Packages:
  * Infant Fare: Mostly Free of Charge (FOC).
  * Seating Policy: Infants are strictly NOT entitled to a seat on the coach; they must sit on the parents' / guardian's lap.
* Bintan & Batam Packages:
  * Agents should check the respective package sales kit / price sheet to verify if specific infant pricing applies.
* Overseas Packages:
  * Always verify the sales kit / itinerary to check whether specific infant pricing is provided or if the child is categorized as Child No Bed (CNB) / Child With Bed (CWB).
  * If infant pricing is unlisted or unclear, agents must check with their Team Lead before confirming with the customer.
* Cruise Bookings:
  * Child and infant age definitions and fare rules vary significantly across cruise lines.
  * Agents must always check directly within the specific cruise booking system, as each cruise brand maintains its own distinct age thresholds and price categories.

## 9. TOUR GUIDING LANGUAGES BY DESTINATION

> MANDATORY AGENT VERIFICATION: Language arrangements are subject to seasonal changes, specific departure dates, and local land operator arrangements. Agents MUST ALWAYS double-check the specific tour's sales kit and itinerary to confirm the guide language before giving any confirmation to the customer.

### Malaysia
* Penang, Ipoh, Taiping Group Tour: English or Mandarin speaking (subject to local arrangement). Always check sales kit / itinerary.
* Penang & Betong: English or Mandarin speaking (subject to local arrangement). Always check sales kit / itinerary.
* Best of Sarawak Kuching: English speaking.
* Sarawak Twin Cities (Sibu & Kuching): English speaking.

### East Asia
* China: Mandarin speaking.
* Hong Kong: English speaking for Group Tours.
* Japan: Mandarin speaking (unless otherwise stated in itinerary/sales kit).
* Korea: Mandarin speaking (unless otherwise stated in itinerary/sales kit).
* Macao: Chinese / Mandarin speaking.
* Taiwan: Chinese / Mandarin speaking.

### Southeast Asia
* Cambodia: English speaking tour guide. (Mandarin guide requires a top-up surcharge; always refer to sales kit / itinerary).
* Indonesia: Predominantly English speaking unless explicitly specified in the sales kit / itinerary:
  * Bali, Bandung, Belitung, Medan, Surabaya, Yogyakarta, Labuan Bajo: English speaking tour guide.
  * Pontianak: English or Mandarin speaking tour guide.
* Laos: Chinese or English speaking guide.
* Philippines: English speaking guide.
* Thailand: English speaking guide (unless otherwise stated in itinerary/sales kit).
* Vietnam: Mandarin or English speaking guide depending on departure date (strictly refer to sales kit / itinerary, as certain departures are English and others are Chinese).

### Oceania & Other Destinations
* Australia: English speaking tour guide.
* New Zealand: English speaking tour guide.
* Bhutan: English speaking tour guide.
* Nepal: English speaking guide.
