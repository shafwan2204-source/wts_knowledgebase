# 3. ESCALATION PROTOCOLS & EMERGENCIES

### CASE (Consumers Association of Singapore) Enquiries
- Immediately escalate all correspondence involving CASE to management, regardless of whether previously escalated.

### Customer Complaints Handling
- Mandatory Information:
  - Full Name
  - Contact Number
  - TBF Booking Number (if applicable)
  - Full 4W1H details: What happened, When, Where, How.
- Escalation Destinations:
  - Complaints about Tours / Trips: Escalate to Main Escalation Group (include TBF and Internal Note format).
  - Complaints about Outlet Staff: Escalate directly to Team Lead (with Internal Note format).
  - Complaints about Bus Drivers: Escalate directly to Team Lead (with Internal Note format).

### Urgent Customer Situations
- Triggers:
  - Customer unable to check into hotel, cruise, or tour.
  - Bus not showing up AND customer already contacted Ops Team without resolution.
- Action Steps:
  1. Collect customer's name, contact number, and TBF number.
  2. Missing TBF: Collect booking person's passport name and departure date, then search system.
  3. Still cannot find TBF: Escalate without TBF, explicitly stating TBF is missing.
  4. Verification: Spell-back customer name using NATO phonetics / country names; double/triple confirm contact numbers.
  5. Never redirect customer to an outlet if the outlet is closed or not answering calls.

### Day-of-Departure Bus Issues / No-Show
- Customer cannot locate bus on departure day or reports no-show:
  - Contact WTS Travel Malaysia Ops immediately: +65 9297 3646

### WTS9 Cross-Border Service Handling
- Operating Rule: Call centre is from a separate department and does not have access to live cross-border bus status information.
- Standard Customer Response Scripts:
  - Option 1:
    > "Unfortunately, we are from a separate department and do not have access to bus status information. Passengers may only obtain updates through the contact number provided (+65 9781 8060). For any feedback or concerns, please submit them through our website or email tms@wtstravel.com.sg."
  - Option 2:
    > "Dear customer, kindly be informed that cross-border service enquiries are managed by a different team. Please reach out to them via WhatsApp at +65 9781 8060 or email tms@wtstravel.com.sg for further assistance."

# 4. BOOKING POLICIES & FARE RULES

### Coach Fares, Estimates & General Hotline Policy
- 2-Way Coach Price: Quote bundled 2-way fare only. Do not split departure vs. return unless customer specifically insists.
- HOTLINE RESTRICTION: Coach-only pricing is strictly NOT available via our general hotline. Hotline agents may only help check slot availability.
- Standard Customer Script for Coach Pricing Queries:
  > "We regret to inform you that coach-only pricing is not available via our general hotline. Nevertheless, we can still help check whether slots are available. Should you wish to confirm the pricing, kindly contact or visit your nearest outlet at: https://www.wtstravel.com.sg/outlets/"
- Indicative Estimated Coach Fares (Reference Only):
  - 28-Seater Coach (Genting): Estimated SGD $90 – $110 per pax (2-way).
  - 21-Seater Platinum Coach (Genting): Estimated SGD $120 – $140 per pax (2-way, to Genting only).
    - Available pickup points strictly limited to: Tampines, Bukit Panjang, Choa Chu Kang, Sengkang.
    - Conditions: Subject to availability, first-come, first-served.
  - Melaka Coach: Estimated SGD $75 – $95 per pax (2-way).
  - KL Coach: Estimated SGD $85 – $105 per pax (2-way).
  - Desaru Coach: Estimated SGD $53 – $73 per pax (2-way).
  - Legoland Coach: Estimated SGD $29 – $49 per pax (2-way).

### Dream Ride Charter Rates & Policy
- Indicative Vehicle Types & Estimates:
  - Melaka:
    - 7-Seater Coach: Estimated SGD $750 per way
    - 11-Seater Coach: Estimated SGD $900 per way
  - Johor Bahru (JB):
    - 7-Seater Coach: Estimated SGD $420 per way
    - 11-Seater Coach: Estimated SGD $500 per way
  - Kuala Lumpur (KL) / Genting:
    - 7-Seater Coach: Estimated SGD $1,000 per way
    - 11-Seater Coach: Estimated SGD $1,100 per way
- Pricing Conditions:
  - Price subject to final confirmation from Dream Ride Team.
  - Price subject to changes based on travel dates.
- Booking / Quotation Process:
  - Instruct customer to WhatsApp +65 9781 8060 (Dream Ride Team) for final pricing and official quotation.
  - Remind customer to provide the specific travel date and vehicle type required to charter.

### Sales Kit & Document Sharing Rules
- Oversea Tour: DO NOT share sales kit. Provide itinerary only.
- Overland Tour: Allowed to share farelist and itinerary.
- Bus Tickets: DO NOT share bus farelist.
- Cruises: Allowed to share farelist and itinerary.
- Club Med: Allowed to share proposal.

### Last Minute Booking Deadlines
- Less than 3 days to departure: Inform customer it is "Subject to availability".
- Less than 1 day to departure: Inform customer "System already closed".

### Availability Commitments
- Always state "Subject to confirmation".
- NEVER guarantee or commit availability. Advise that availability is accurate only at the instant of query and subject to final confirmation.

### Coach Seating & Grouping Policy
- Same Coach Guarantee: Only guaranteed if booked under the same invoice (identical travel date, pickup point, and drop-off point).
- Separate Invoices: Cannot guarantee same coach; submit as a request only.
- Seat Allocation: Assigned exclusively by the bus captain during boarding/check-in. Call centre and outlets do not assign seats.

### Air Ticket Markup Surcharge
- Full Service Carriers (SIA, Malaysia Airlines) per passenger:
  - Fare <= SGD $1,000: Add SGD $35 markup
  - Fare >= SGD $1,001: Add 3% markup of total fare
- Low-Cost Carriers (Scoot, AirAsia) per passenger:
  - Any amount: Add flat SGD $25 markup
