## 3. ESCALATION PROTOCOLS & EMERGENCIES

### Missing Information & Unlisted Inquiries
* If the knowledge base does not have the answer to a question or specific scenario, agents **must not guess or make assumptions**[cite: 1].
* **Action**: Contact the **Team Lead for clarification** before replying or committing to the customer[cite: 1].

### CASE (Consumers Association of Singapore) Enquiries
* Immediately escalate all correspondence involving CASE to management, regardless of whether it was previously escalated[cite: 1].

### Customer Complaints Handling
* **Mandatory Information to Collect**:
  * Full Name[cite: 1]
  * Contact Number[cite: 1]
  * TBF Booking Number (if applicable)[cite: 1]
  * Full 4W1H details: What happened, When, Where, How[cite: 1]
* **Escalation Routing**:
  * **Complaints about Tours / Trips**: Escalate to Main Escalation Group (include TBF and Internal Note format)[cite: 1].
  * **Complaints about Outlet Staff**: Escalate directly to Team Lead (with Internal Note format)[cite: 1].
  * **Complaints about Bus Drivers**: Escalate directly to Team Lead (with Internal Note format)[cite: 1].

### Urgent Customer Situations
* **Triggers**:
  * Customer unable to check into hotel, cruise, or tour[cite: 1].
  * Bus not showing up AND customer has already contacted Ops Team without resolution[cite: 1].
* **Action Steps**:
  1. Collect customer's name, contact number, and TBF number[cite: 1].
  2. If TBF is missing: Collect booking person's passport name and departure date, then search system[cite: 1].
  3. If TBF still cannot be found: Escalate without TBF, explicitly stating that TBF is missing[cite: 1].
  4. Verification: Spell-back customer name using NATO phonetics / country names; double/triple confirm contact numbers[cite: 1].
  5. Never redirect a customer to an outlet if the outlet is closed or not answering calls[cite: 1].

### Day-of-Departure Bus Issues / No-Show
* If a customer cannot locate their bus on departure day or reports a no-show:
  * Contact WTS Travel Malaysia Ops immediately: **+65 9297 3646**[cite: 1]

### WTS9 Cross-Border Service Handling
* **Operating Rule**: The call centre is from a separate department and does not have access to live cross-border bus status information[cite: 1].

For any feedback or concerns, please submit them through our website or email tms@wtstravel.com.sg."*[cite: 1]

* **HOTLINE RESTRICTION**: Coach-only pricing is **strictly NOT available** via our general hotline. Hotline agents may only help check slot availability[cite: 1].
* **Standard Customer Script for Coach Pricing Queries**:
  > *"We regret to inform you that coach-only pricing is not available via our general hotline. Nevertheless, we can still help check whether slots are available. Should you wish to confirm the pricing, kindly contact or visit your nearest outlet at: https://www.wtstravel.com.sg/outlets/"*[cite: 1]

### Watersports & Optional Tour Add-Ons
* Unless specifically priced as an optional tour add-on, watersports are usually purchased directly from the hotel water sports centre.

### Sales Kit & Document Sharing Rules
* **Oversea Tour**: DO NOT share sales kit. Provide itinerary only[cite: 1].
* **Overland Tour**: Allowed to share farelist and itinerary[cite: 1].
* **Bus Tickets**: DO NOT share bus farelist[cite: 1].
* **Cruises**: Allowed to share farelist and itinerary[cite: 1].
* **Club Med**: Allowed to share proposal[cite: 1].

### Last Minute Booking Deadlines
* **Less than 3 days to departure**: Inform customer it is *"Subject to availability"*[cite: 1].
* **Less than 1 day to departure**: Inform customer *"System already closed"*[cite: 1].

### Availability Commitments
* Always state *"Subject to confirmation"*[cite: 1].
* **NEVER guarantee or commit availability**. Advise that availability is accurate only at the instant of query and subject to final confirmation[cite: 1].

### Coach Seating & Grouping Policy
* **Same Coach Guarantee**: Only guaranteed if booked under the same invoice (identical travel date, pickup point, and drop-off point)[cite: 1].
* **Separate Invoices**: Cannot guarantee same coach; submit as a request only[cite: 1].
* **Seat Allocation**: Assigned exclusively by the bus captain during boarding/check-in. Call centre and outlets do not pre-assign seats[cite: 1].

### Air Ticket Markup Surcharge
* **Full Service Carriers (SIA, Malaysia Airlines) per passenger**:
  * Base Fare $\le$ SGD $1,000$: Add SGD $35 markup[cite: 1]
  * Base Fare $\ge$ SGD $1,001$: Add 3% markup of total fare[cite: 1]
* **Low-Cost Carriers (Scoot, AirAsia) per passenger**:
  * Any amount: Add flat SGD $25 markup[cite: 1]
